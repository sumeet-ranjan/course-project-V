# Quarterly Highlights 

 Value of Area Booked recorded at Rs 454.16 Crores (Q3FY25) vs Rs 173.89 Crores (Q3FY24) and Rs. 672.54 Crores in Q2FY25. Area Booked in Q2FY25 was higher due to launch of Ashiana Amarah Phase 4 in Gurugram. Q3FY25 had good bookings across projects. 

 Average realization price of Q3FY25 at Rs. 6,705 vs Rs. 5,189 in Q3FY24. The same was Rs 9,223 in Q2FY25, higher realization was mainly driven by bookings in Ashiana Amarah Phase 4 . 

 Equivalent area constructed (EAC) at 5.19 lakhs square feet in Q3FY25 vs 4.77 lakhs square feet in Q3FY24. EAC was 6.01 lakhs square feet in Q2FY25, QoQ decline by 13.64% due to imposition of GRAP related restrictions in Delhi-NCR. 

 We continue to maintain our guidance of Rs. 2,000 Crores of Pre Sales for FY25. However, this will depend on the velocity of bookings in the launch of Ashiana Amarah Phase 5 in Q4FY25. 

 Third senior living project launched in Chennai by the name ‘Ashiana Swarang’ located in Nemmeli. Apart from that Ashiana Ekansh (Phase 4) in Jaipur and Ashiana Amodh (Phase 2) in Pune were launched in the quarter. 

 Handover started for Tarang Phase 4A in Bhiwadi and Umang Phase 6 in Jaipur. 

 Total Revenue reported at Rs 139.93 Crores(Q3FY25) vs Rs 59.53 Crores(Q2FY25).Increase by 135% driven by higher deliveries in Q3FY25. 

 PAT for Q3FY25 at positive Rs.10.89 Crores vs negative Rs. 7.55 Crores in Q2FY25. Profit attributable to higher deliveries in Q3FY25 . PAT had a one-time impact of Rs. 5 Crores payout wrt. GST matter of Treehouse Hotel(shown as an exceptional line item in P&L). 

 Pre-Tax operating cash flow achieved at Rs. 120.42 Crores in Q3 FY25. Cash flows continue to be healthy due to higher collections driven by better sales across projects, in general. © Ashiana Housing Limited, All Rights Reserved. 6

# 9MFY25 Highlights 

 Value of Area Booked recorded at Rs 1,362.03 Crores (9MFY25) vs Rs 935.68 Crores (9MFY24), an upside of 45.56%, increase mainly due to area booked Rs. 503.68 of Ashiana Amarah Phase 4 and Rs. 218.68 of Ashiana Ekansh Phase 3 and 4. 

 EAC at 16.11 lakhs square feet in 9MFY25 vs 13.71 lakhs square feet in 9MFY24, increased by 17.50%. 

 We continue to maintain our guidance of Rs. 2,000 Crores of Pre Sales for FY25. However, this will depend on the velocity of bookings in the launch of Ashiana Amarah Phase 5 in Q4FY25. 

 Total Revenue reported at Rs 327.97 Crores(9MFY25) vs Rs 669.56 Crores (9MFY24). Lower revenues due to lower deliveries. 

 PAT for 9MFY25 at negative Rs. 2.10 Crores vs positive Rs. 66.02 Crores in 9MFY24, lower margins impacted by lower deliveries & also due to mix of projects delivered. In 9MFY25, Shubham phase 4 (Chennai), Tarang Phase 4A (Bhiwadi) and Umang Phase 6 (Jaipur) were handed over. PAT had a one-time impact of Rs. 5 Crores payout wrt. GST matter of Treehouse Hotel(shown as an exceptional line item in P&L). 

 Pre – Tax Operating Cashflow for 9MFY25 recorded at Rs 273.53 Crores vs 212.27 Crores in 9MFY24. Cash flows continue to be healthy (in line/better than last year) due to higher collections driven by better sales, across projects. 

 Launches: Ashiana Amarah (Phase 4) in Gurugram, Ashiana Malhar (Phase3) in Pune, One44 (Phase 2) in Jaipur, Ashiana Advik (Phase 2) in Bhiwadi, Ashiana Ekansh (Phase 4) in Jaipur, Ashiana Amodh (Phase 2) in Pune and Ashiana Swarang (Phase 1) in Chennai.
