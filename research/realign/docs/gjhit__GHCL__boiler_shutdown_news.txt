## GHCL announces annual maintenance of boiler at soda ash plant

This is expected to result in a total production shortfall of approximately 14,000 tons

*    By **ICN Bureau** | August 19, 2024 

*   [](https://www.facebook.com/sharer/sharer.php?u=www.indianchemicalnews.com%2Fchemical%2Fghcl-announces-annual-maintenance-of-boiler-at-soda-ash-plant-22927)[](https://twitter.com/share?text=GHCL+announces+annual+maintenance+of+boiler+at+soda+ash+plant&url=www.indianchemicalnews.com%2Fchemical%2Fghcl-announces-annual-maintenance-of-boiler-at-soda-ash-plant-22927)[](https://web.whatsapp.com/send?text=www.indianchemicalnews.com/chemical/ghcl-announces-annual-maintenance-of-boiler-at-soda-ash-plant-22927)[](https://www.linkedin.com/sharing/share-offsite/?url=www.indianchemicalnews.com/chemical/ghcl-announces-annual-maintenance-of-boiler-at-soda-ash-plant-22927)

![Image 4](https://www.indianchemicalnews.com/public/uploads/news/2024/08/22927/GHCL2.jpg)

GHCl Limited announced that the annual maintenance activities of the boiler at the company's soda ash plant are scheduled to take place from September 16, 2024 for a period of two weeks.

This is expected to result in a total production shortfall of approximately 14,000 tons. The purpose of these activities are to carry out essential maintenance tasks to ensure the efficient and safe operations of Soda Ash plant.

During this period, the company wants to assure all stakeholders that there is a sufficient reserve stock of soda ash to meet the customers' requirements therefore, there will be no adverse impact on our supply commitments during the aforementioned period.
