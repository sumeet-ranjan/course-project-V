# Gaurs get tenders from NBCC for Amrapali projects

By [Realtynmore](https://realtynmore.com/author/admin/)• Mar 26, 2021 

![Image 4: Gaur](https://realtynmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Gaur-1024x1024.jpg)

[**Gaurs Group**](https://www.facebook.com/GaursonsIndiaPrivateLimited/) has been awarded four tenders from state-run NBCC worth Rs 2,124 crore for stuck Amrapali projects – **Verona Heights**, **Heartbeat City** and **Amrapali Centurian Park** with a total inventory of 10,964 flats.

![Image 5: Sarthak Gaur](https://www.realtynmore.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Sarthak-Gaur-300x300.jpg)“Having invested in construction over the years, we are increasing our presence in the **infrastructure segment** and are willing to take Government projects,” said **Sarthak Gaur**, **Director, Gaurs Group**.

“Since our expertise is **real estate**, taking **Amrapali projects** was a natural option. We are looking to increase our construction portfolio to Rs 7,000 crore in five years”, he added.

The Group is not executing any large-scale project currently. It wants to diversify towards infrastructure.

“The Government has shifted its focus on the **infrastructure segment** and as a Group, we have also decided to diversify. We have no large-scale **real estate** projects as of now and we have sold the majority of our under construction inventory. In the Amrapali projects, we are only executing it and responsibility of sales lies with NBCC,” said Gaur further.

The group’s wholly-owned subsidiary, **Gaursons****Hitech Infrastructure Pvt Ltd**, is executing these projects.

NBCC is involved in the completion of 23 housing projects of **Amrapali**. The estimated cost of completing these projects is about Rs 8,500 crore.

The state-run company has divided the stuck **Amrapali** projects into three categories and delivery of flats will start soon.

“We raise a monthly bill to NBCC depending on the construction done. We are meeting the construction cost through internal accruals and bank guarantees. The projects will be completed within 36 months of the allotment of the tender,” Gaur concluded.
