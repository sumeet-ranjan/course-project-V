Consolidated Performance 

The production and sales stood at 1.99 mt (+3% yoy) and 1.90 mt (+5% yoy) respectively. Consolidated gross revenues for the quarter came at INR 13,707 Cr (+0.1% yoy). Share of exports was 7% in Q3FY25. Adjusted EBITDA was INR 2,133 Cr (-24% yoy) adjusted for one-off FX gain of INR 51 Cr during the quarter. Reported PAT at INR 951 Cr down -51% yoy. 

JSP ’s net debt stood at INR 13,551 Cr as on 31 st Dec ’24 vs. INR 12,464 Cr as on 30 th Sep ’2 4. Net debt to EBITDA increased to 1.40x as on 31 st Dec ’24 vs 1.21x as on 30 th Sep ’2 4. The planned expansion projects are progressing well as per the stipulated timelines, supported by strong company financials. The total capex for the quarter was INR 2,857 Cr largely driven by the expansion projects at Angul. 

Industry overview 

India steel production for the quarter stood at 38.4 mt up 6% qoq and apparent steel consumption grew by 4% sequentially. Exports grew 44% qoq to 1.8 mt while imports continued to trend down, declining 13% qoq to 2.8 mt. As a result, India continues to remain net importer. JSP - Financial Results Q3FY25
