# NBCC to act as Project Management Consultant for two projects in New Delhi

[NBCC (India) Ltd.](https://www.moneyworks4me.com/indianstocks/small-cap/capital-goods-industrial-consumables/engineering-consultancy/nbcc-india/company-info) | [Engineering Consultancy](https://www.moneyworks4me.com/comp-peer/index/index/type/industry/fid/208801) | [Capital Goods & Industrial Consumables](https://www.moneyworks4me.com/comp-peer/index/index/type/sector/fid/208801) | [BSE: 534309](https://www.moneyworks4me.com/indianstocks/small-cap/capital-goods-industrial-consumables/engineering-consultancy/nbcc-india/company-info) | [NSE: NBCC](https://www.moneyworks4me.com/indianstocks/small-cap/capital-goods-industrial-consumables/engineering-consultancy/nbcc-india/company-info)

27 Feb 2018[Evaluate](https://www.moneyworks4me.com/indianstocks/small-cap/capital-goods-industrial-consumables/engineering-consultancy/nbcc-india/company-info#ten-year-xray-anchor-link)

NBCC (India) has awarded the contract of GPRA colonies at Netaji Nagar (Pkg-I) & Sarojini Nagar (Pkg-I), New Delhi on Design, Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) basis including operations & maintenance wherein NBCC is working as Project Management Consultant.

The company has awarded redevelopment project of GPRA colony (Pkg-I) at Netaji Nagar, New Delhi to Shapoorji Pallonji & Co for around Rs 1039.32 crore with completion period of 24 months.

The company has awarded another project of redevelopment of GPRA colony (Pkg-I) Sarojni Nagar, New Delhi to Girdhari Lal Constructions for around Rs 847.77 crore with completion period of 24 months.

NBCC (India) is one of the few public sector companies engaged in the business of project management consultancy services for civil construction projects (PMC), civil infrastructure for power sector and real estate development.

# About MoneyWorks4Me

 MoneyWorks4Me has been helping investors make better investment decisions since **2008**. 

**MoneyWorks4Me Investment Advisers** is the trade/brand name of **The Alchemists Ark Pvt. Ltd.**, a SEBI-registered non-individual Investment Adviser **(Registration No. INA000013323)**.

 We began in 2008 as an equity research company, helping investors identify high-quality Indian businesses and understand what they were worth through **deep fundamental research** and **intrinsic-value-based investing**.

 Over time, we learnt that successful investing requires much more than identifying good stocks. 

 Investors also need to know how much to invest, how to allocate money across different assets, how to construct a strong portfolio, **when to act** and, equally importantly, **when not to**.

 That understanding shaped our evolution from an equity research company into a broader investment company built around **research, portfolio strategy, technology and human judgement**.

## One Investing Philosophy. Different Ways to Benefit From It.

 Today, our capabilities extend across**Indian direct equities, mutual funds, global equities, fixed income, gold and REITs** and other suitable investment opportunities.

 We do not see these as separate investment products. **They are different building blocks of one portfolio.**

 The starting point is the investor: their goals, required returns, time horizon, ability to take risk and the role each asset needs to play.

**Asset allocation** determines where the money should go.**Research and valuation** guide what we own. **Portfolio construction** determines how much we own.Periodic review and rebalancing help keep the portfolio aligned with its purpose.

 This investment philosophy is applied in different ways across MoneyWorks4Me. 

 Through **Omega Freedom** and **Omega Legacy**, we work closely with investors who want comprehensive investment advice and guidance in managing their portfolios.

 Through **Third Rock Wealth**, we bring the same investment thinking and research depth to investors who prefer mutual funds as their route to wealth creation.

## Research at the Core

 We look at business quality, financial strength, competitive advantage, management, growth prospects and risks. But we always consider these alongside valuation.

 A good company does not automatically make a good investment at every price.

Our **Quality-at-a-Reasonable-Price** approach seeks to bring quality and valuation together. 

## Start With the Portfolio

We do not begin by asking which stock, fund or asset looks attractive.

**We begin by asking what the portfolio needs.**

Indian equities may provide long-term growth. Global equities can broaden diversification. Fixed income can provide stability and liquidity. Gold has a different role to play. 

**Every investment should earn its place in the portfolio.**

## Focus on Process, Not Prediction

 Markets cannot be predicted consistently. A sound investment process can be followed consistently.

 We therefore focus on what can be controlled: **asset allocation, quality, valuation, diversification, position sizing, risk management, review and taking action when required.**

## Let Compounding Do the Heavy Lifting

 Long-term wealth is not created only by finding investments with spectacular returns. 

It comes from putting money to work intelligently, earning **satisfactory inflation-beating returns across the portfolio** and staying invested long enough for compounding to make a meaningful difference.

## Technology Has Always Been Part of MoneyWorks4Me

 Technology has been integral to MoneyWorks4Me from the beginning, from our **financial X-Rays and valuation tools** to portfolio analytics and research systems. 

 Investing still involves context, uncertainty and decisions for which there is not always an obvious answer.

## Investors Should Understand Why

 Our advisory business operates in a**fiduciary capacity**, and the investor's interests come first.

 We believe investors should understand the reasoning behind important investment decisions rather than simply follow recommendations. 

 That is the thinking behind our **Do-It-With-Me** approach.

It is designed to help investors remain in control of their money while benefiting from **professional research, process and perspective**. 

## Our Vision

Our vision is to build a **trusted, research-powered investment company** that helps investors make calm, informed and well-reasoned decisions throughout their investing journey. 

 Markets will change. Investment opportunities will change. Technology will change how investors access information and advice.

But the principles of successful investing are far more enduring:own quality assets at sensible prices, build portfolios intelligently, manage risk and give compounding enough time to work.

 That is the investment discipline MoneyWorks4Me has been building since 2008 and continues to strengthen for the future.
