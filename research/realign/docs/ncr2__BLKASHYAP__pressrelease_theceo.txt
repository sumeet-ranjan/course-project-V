# B L Kashyap & Sons Limited secures new order worth Rs. 250 crores approx. from Anant Raj Limited. The current order book stands at ap-prox Rs. 3796 crores



B L Kashyap & Sons Limited awarded civil and structural works for The Estate Residences, a group housing project in Gurugram



Shweta Singh



2 min read

*   
*   
*   
*   
*   
*   Copied 
*   

New Delhi…November 07, 2024...B L Kashyap & Sons Ltd. (BSE: 532719 NSE: BLKASHYAP), one of the leading Engineering, Procurement and Construction(EPC) Company has secured a new order worth Rs. 250 crores approx. from Anant Raj Limited. The company received this order for the civil and structural works to constructThe Estate Residences- a group housing project in Gurugram. With this addition, the current order book stands at approx. Rs. 3796 crores excluding GST.

Mr. Vineet Kashyap, Managing Director, B L Kashyap & Sons Ltd. said, “This new order from Anant Raj Limited reflects our continued commitment to delivering high-quality and structurally robust developments. This project further strengthens our order book and underscores our dedication to driving growth and innovation in the EPC sector. We look forward to leveraging our technical expertise to execute this project seamlessly, contributing to the region's infrastructure landscape and setting new benchmarks in the industry.”



## About B L Kashyap:

B L Kashyap & Sons Ltd. (BLK) is one of the leading Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) Company. The company has a presence in 12 cities across 8 states in India. With Three decades of expertise, BLK has completed over 250 projects and more than 140 million sq. ft. The portfolio spread includes Railways, IT Campuses, Commercial Spaces, Malls, Hotels, Residential Complexes, Institutions, Factories and Manufacturing Facilities, Healthcareand Transportation. BLK has a strong workforce of 1200+ engineers & professionals

For further information on the company: www.blkashyap.com
